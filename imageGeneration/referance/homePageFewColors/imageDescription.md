**Core Style Descriptors**

* **Primary Medium & Technique:** Vintage woodcut, linocut, fine cross-hatching, stipple engraving, ink hatching, scraperboard/scratchboard illustration.
* **Color Palette:** Warm vintage parchment, aged beige/kraft paper background, deep charcoal/black ink, subtle muted accents of ochre gold, warm amber, and burnt sienna.
* **Tone & Mood:** Gritty historical realism, dark folklore, Nordic rustic, weathered, mythic, textured, editorial storybook.
* **Lighting & Shading:** High-contrast chiaroscuro, dense linear cross-hatching for shadows, clean paper-negative space for highlights, subtle warm light sources (lantern glow, hearth fire).

---

**Ready-to-Use Prompt Snippets**

* **Full Style Description (for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion):**

> *A vintage woodcut illustration of [SUBJECT], detailed ink linocut and fine cross-hatching technique, dark Nordic folklore style, high-contrast black ink on aged beige textured parchment paper, subtle muted ochre and amber color accents, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, gritty rustic realism, antique storybook engraving, master linework.*

* **Short Style Tags / Modifiers:**

> `woodcut print, vintage engraving, cross-hatching linework, Nordic folklore illustration, aged parchment texture, black ink drawing, rustic sepia tones, chiaroscuro stippling, linocut aesthetic`

* **Negative Prompt (Useful for Stable Diffusion / Midjourney `--no`):**

> `photorealistic, 3D render, glossy, neon, vibrant modern colors, smooth shading, digital blur, airbrushed, cartoon, anime, bright white background`
